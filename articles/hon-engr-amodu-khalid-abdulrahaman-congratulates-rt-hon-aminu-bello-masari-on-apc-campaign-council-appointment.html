CONGRATULATORY MESSAGE ON YOUR APPOINTMENT TO THE APC PRESIDENTIAL CAMPAIGN COUNCIL

​His Excellency,

Rt. Hon. Aminu Bello Masari, CFR

Former Executive Governor of Katsina State & Former Speaker, House of Representatives

Deputy Director-General (Parliamentarian Mobilisation), APC Presidential Campaign Council

​Your Excellency,

​On behalf of the Management and Staff of AE Dynamic Synergy Ltd, I extend our heartiest congratulations to you on your well-deserved appointment as the Deputy Director-General (Parliamentarian Mobilisation) of the All Progressives Congress (APC) Presidential Campaign Council for the re-election of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, GCFR.

​This high-level assignment is a clear testament to your stellar track record as a seasoned statesman, an astute political strategist, and a dedicated leader whose contributions to legislative governance and executive leadership continue to inspire national trust. Having served the nation with distinction as Speaker of the House of Representatives and as Governor of Katsina State, your deep wealth of experience makes you uniquely suited to galvanize parliamentarians and key stakeholders nationwide toward securing a resounding victory. 

​We are confident that your dynamic leadership will inject vast momentum into the campaign council's objectives, fostering grassroots alignment and legislative coalition across the country.

​As you step into this vital national role, we pray that Almighty God grants you exceptional wisdom, strength, and success in driving this mandate to a triumphant outcome.

​Please accept, Your Excellency, the assurances of our highest esteem and warm regards.

​Signed:

​Hon. Engr. Amodu Khalid Abdulrahaman FHIM ,ACIS

Managing Director/

Chief Executive Officer, AE Dynamic Synergy Ltd
